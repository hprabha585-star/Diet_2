How the 55 days work

Three roles, one daily rhythm

Your coach configures the day. You execute it. The system just keeps score — nobody's syncing spreadsheets by hand.

Every morning

Coach sets the day

Your fasting window, your meals with macros, and a short checklist of habits — tailored to your weight, your goals, and how last week went.

All day

You execute

A live countdown tells you whether you're fasting or eating. You tick off meals, log water, and mark habits as you go — no logging calories from memory at 9pm.

Every evening

The cohort sees it

Your adherence, streak, and points update on the leaderboard. Your coach sees who's slipping before it becomes a pattern.
